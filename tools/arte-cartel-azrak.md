# Cartel de misiones del reino de Azrak

Archivo integrado: `assets/images/ui/presentacion-mision-azrak-v1.png`.

Generado con ImageGen, usando `presentacion-mision-hielo-v1.png` como referencia de composición. Se aplica a las diez misiones del mundo 5; los textos siguen siendo HTML accesible y se adaptan a cada misión. Incluido en la caché sin conexión v245.

## Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a new landscape illustrated mission announcement board for world 5, the volcanic dark fantasy Kingdom of Azrak, in a family word adventure game. Reference is only the layout and polished storybook 3D quality of the ice world announcement board: replace all ice, snow and blue decorations. A frontal symmetrical wide black obsidian and weathered dark basalt plaque, ornate aged bronze trim, glowing subtle orange magma cracks around the OUTER edges, a small amethyst eclipse crest centered at top, fortress-like corner carvings, a few drifting embers. Large uninterrupted blank dark warm charcoal center covering about 70 percent width and 65 percent height, low texture and no glare so cream mission text can be overlaid in code. Frame readable and elegant rather than frightening. Full board in frame, landscape 3:2, dark nearly black background. No lettering, no words, no numbers, no characters, no skulls, no watermark. Colors charcoal, bronze, restrained lava orange, small violet accents.

## Validación

Las diez misiones se verificaron en navegador a 390×844, 844×390 y 1365×768: imagen cargada, texto completo sin desplazamiento y contenido dentro de pantalla. Capturas revisadas en vertical y horizontal. Suite existente: 44 pruebas aprobadas.
